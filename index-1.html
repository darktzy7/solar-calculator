ABOUT

Simpleng calculator ito para sa pag-size ng off-grid solar system. Gabay lang ang resulta at panimulang batayan; ipa-check pa rin sa lisensyadong electrician o solar installer bago bumili.

  • Solar array = Daily energy ÷ efficiency ÷ peak sun hours
  • Battery bank = Daily energy × backup days ÷ DoD
  • Batteries = (System V ÷ battery V) in series × parallel strings
  • Charge controller = Array W ÷ system V × 1.25 safety
  • Inverter = Peak load × 1.25 safety
